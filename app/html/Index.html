SMOSH croissant Recipe


The most awesome smosh croissant receipe that everyone should try and make and also comment below about what other recipes we should try

Ingredients

Dough

  • Flour: 510 g all-purpose flour
  • Sugar: 65 g granulated suga
  • Salt: 12 g fine sea salt
  • Yeast: 10 g instant yeast
  • Liquids: 144 g cold water + 144 g cold whole milk
  • Butter: 43 g unsalted butter, softened

Layers & Coating

  • Butter Block: 285 g cold, high-fat unsalted European-style butter
  • Egg Wash: 1 egg + 1 yolk whisked with a pinch of salt

instructions

  1. The Dough: Mix all dough ingredients into a stiff ball. Wrap and chill for 4+ hours.
  2. The Butter Block: Roll the 285 g cold butter between parchment paper into a 7.5-inch flat square. Chill until firm but pliable.
  3. The Lock-In: Roll dough into an 11-inch square. Place the butter block diagonally in the center. Fold dough corners over the butter to seal it inside completely.
  4. The Folds: Roll the dough package into an 8x24-inch rectangle. Fold into thirds like a letter. Chill 1 hour. Repeat this rolling, folding, and chilling cycle two more times. Rest in the fridge overnight.
  5. The Shaping: Roll dough out to a 1/4-inch thick rectangle. Cut into elongated triangles. Roll tightly from base to tip.
  6. Baking: Rise at room temperature for 2–3 hours until puffy. Brush with egg wash. Bake at 425°F (218°C) for 10 minutes, lower to 375°F (190°C), and bake 10 more minutes until golden.
